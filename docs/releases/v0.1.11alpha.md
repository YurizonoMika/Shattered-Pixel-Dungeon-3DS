## 简体中文

按 pixeldungeon.wiki 的 Shattered 主命名空间和固定 v4.0.2 源码展开机制复盘，记录页面修订号、冲突和完整系统差异。此次是基础规则修正，尚未完成全游戏对齐。

- 新局统一20点生命、10点力量；力量药水永久+1力量。经验按 `5+5×当前等级` 结算，上限30级；怪物有经验等级上限。
- 食物恢复饱食度并花费3回合。饥饿、挨饿、自然回血和治疗药水的持续治疗都成为可保存状态。
- 修正已支持武器和护甲的档位、基础伤害、力量需求、减伤和不足力量惩罚。保存累计行动耗时，区分快慢武器、重甲移动和迅捷移动；逐角色调度仍不完整。
- 撤去自定房间刷怪上限，恢复尺寸权重和追加生成尝试。已知陷阱参与绕行，探图揭示陷阱，警报影响整层怪物。

存档升级至V11；可导入V9/V10，旧档缺失的力量为10、饥饿为0。旧档无法还原已消耗力量药水，建议备份并开新局验证。V11不能降级读取。

验证：800层生成与补给检查零失败，规则边界、战斗、补怪、交互、存档及陷阱回归通过。CIA已在Azahar安装启动，标题显示本版本；HOME菜单名称和简介不含版本号。未进行真机测试。详细差异见[机制复盘](https://github.com/YurizonoMika/Shattered-Pixel-Dungeon-3DS/blob/main/docs/wiki-mechanics-audit.md)。

## English

Begins a source-backed mechanics audit using the Wiki's Shattered namespace and pinned v4.0.2 code. Page revisions, conflicts and outstanding systems are recorded. This release does not claim complete game parity.

- New runs start at 20 HP and 10 STR. Strength potions add one permanent STR. XP thresholds are `5+5×level`, with a level-30 cap and per-monster XP caps.
- Food restores satiety and takes three turns. Hunger, starvation, natural regeneration and potion healing over time are persistent.
- Corrects supported equipment tiers, base ranges, requirements, DR and strength penalties. Fractional action costs distinguish weapon and movement speeds; the full actor scheduler remains incomplete.
- Removes native room occupancy caps in favor of size weights and second-spawn attempts. Navigation avoids visible traps, mapping reveals traps, and alarms affect the whole floor.

V11 imports V9/V10 with missing STR initialized to 10 and hunger to 0. Consumed strength potions cannot be reconstructed from old saves. Back up and start fresh for comparisons. V11 cannot be loaded by older builds.

Validation: 800 generated floors passed supply and route checks. Rule boundaries, combat, respawn, interactions, saves and trap regressions passed. CIA installed and booted in Azahar with the correct title version; HOME Menu metadata remains version-free. No hardware testing was performed. See the [mechanics audit](https://github.com/YurizonoMika/Shattered-Pixel-Dungeon-3DS/blob/main/docs/wiki-mechanics-audit.md) for remaining differences.
