## 简体中文

- 按房间面积限制初始生成和补怪，小房间达到上限后会跳过。补怪失败时保留当前轮到的怪物类型，下一回合重试。
- 增加飞镖、毒气、警报、寒霜、火焰和电击陷阱。触发时显示名称，已触发的陷阱会变暗。
- 毒气云会扩散并持续六回合，警报会发出红色波纹，唤醒附近怪物并把它们引向触发点。图块、特效和六类陷阱的区域权重采用 3DS 版实现。

## English

- Initial spawns and respawns respect room size. A failed respawn keeps the next monster in the rotation and retries on the following turn.
- Darts, toxic gas, alarms, frost, fire and shock have distinct trap markers. Activation displays the trap's name and leaves a dimmed marker behind.
- Toxic gas spreads and lingers for six turns. Alarms send out red waves and draw nearby monsters to the trigger tile. The artwork, effects and regional weights are 3DS adaptations.
