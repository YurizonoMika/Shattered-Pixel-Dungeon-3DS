## 简体中文

存档选择界面支持删除存档。选中已有存档后按 **X**，或点对应卡片的「删除」。确认框会显示槽位、英雄、楼层和等级，按 **A** 永久删除或按 **B** 取消，也可触屏选择。三个槽位都占用时，腾出空位后即可开始新游戏。

## English

You can now delete a save from the save selection screen. Select an occupied slot and press **X**, or tap **删除** on its card. The confirmation shows the slot, hero, floor, and level. Press **A** to delete permanently or **B** to cancel, or use the touch screen. Free a slot before starting a new game if all three are occupied.
