## 简体中文

修复五个区域的黑水问题。恢复 v4.0.2 的原版水面纹理，水纹以每秒 5 像素滚动，岸边和战争迷雾叠加在水面上方。

已在 Azahar 检查五个区域的水面、岸边和雾遮罩，并确认水纹在角色不动时也会滚动。3DSX 已运行验证，CIA 安装成功。存档格式与 v0.1.1alpha 相同。

## English

Fixed black water in all five regions by restoring the original v4.0.2 water textures. Water scrolls at five pixels per second beneath shoreline tiles and fog of war.

Verified water, shoreline, and fog in all five regions in Azahar, including scrolling while the hero stands still. The 3DSX ran successfully and the CIA installed successfully. The save format is unchanged from v0.1.1alpha.
