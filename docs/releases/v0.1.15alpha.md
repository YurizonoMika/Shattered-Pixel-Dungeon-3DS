# v0.1.15alpha development build

This is unpublished work against Shattered v4.0.2, commit
`57a4e06a4caf162446d1c28caa7983f0493fecf0`. Catalog coverage does not establish
complete behavioral parity.

The artifact catalog contains all 13 generator entries. Eleven ordinary
artifacts are unique within a run; the Rogue and Cleric start with their class
artifacts. Rings and artifacts share two accessory rows. Equipped artifacts
offer actions, feeding and removal. Sandals accept different seeds in groups
of 3/6/9, then permit changing their imbued seed. The armband can attempt theft
of selected shop stock. The rose accepts suitable known, uncursed equipment
and supports directing its ghost. Ghost equipment, independent damage state,
cross-floor ownership and regeneration are persistent.

Foresight now uses the upstream cone arc and Ballistica rays, adjacent-target
extension, distance limit and fractional charge debit (including the minimum-
charge diagonal debt). New terrain, secret traps/doors and unseen enemies/heaps
grant discovery XP. Timed awareness follows enemies and retains heap positions
by depth; it survives saving and expires while away from the relevant floor.
Warnings require visible hidden traps and fire when danger is first detected.
The native heap model still groups loose items and heap-like features by cell;
it does not preserve the upstream identity of a newly created heap replacing
an already seen heap on the same cell. All buffs still share the native clock.

There are 17 weapon enchantments, 10 weapon curses, 13 armor glyphs and 8 armor
curses. Enchantment stones, enchantment scrolls and arcane styli select equipment
including equipped items. Upgrades, curse removal, drops and floor archives
retain the relevant item state. Holy effects now follow the item proc and
Trinity imprint once per hit; a Crystal imprint has separate durability and
cannot destroy the real weapon. Crossbow procs retain their own state during
dart attacks, and deferred enchantments can coexist. Individual proc order,
immunity and status interactions still need further upstream comparisons.

The seed catalog has twelve entries, including quest-only rotberry. Plant
corrections share the pinned nine-effect cure list between Mageroyal and healing
potions, retaining burning, chill and paralysis. Starflower grants 30-turn Bless;
Blindweed applies movement-only Cripple rather than Chill. Sungrass and Earthroot
retain separate position anchors and upstream final-tick healing/absorption.
Reapplication retains longer poison, blindness and freezing durations.
Swiftthistle now has independent TimeBubble state: seven initial time points,
the upstream zero/expiry tolerance, queued plant/trap presses, offensive
detachment and floor-transition disarming. Queued cells and remaining time
persist. Monster bubbles consume that monster's actions rather than granting
Haste. Trinity's hourglass spirit uses this same bubble with its artifact level.
Mageroyal and healing potions preserve already active Magical Sleep, which is
separate from Drowsy. Warden effects, full Frost behavior, immunities and
elemental item destruction remain incomplete.
Icecap now applies independent Frost rather than Paralysis. Damage (including
zero damage) detaches Frost, water thaw leaves five turns of Chill, and movement
and attack costs follow the pinned remaining-duration Chill speed formula.
Frost persists independently of existing paralysis. Potion shattering, frozen
meat, full immunity/resistance tables and other Frost sources remain incomplete.
Delayed effects still execute
on the native scheduler rather than the upstream independent VFX actor;
some offensive dispel hooks remain partial. Hourglass freeze now spends one
charge on activation and debits further charges per two action units, retaining
unused charges on cancellation. Fractional tolerance, full-action expiry,
queued traps, bubble priority and unequipping have runtime coverage. Stasis
compensates hunger below the starving threshold and lets healing, existing
statuses and monster buffs advance while protecting the hero. Its charge,
duration and hunger rules pass 4,510 extracted Java cases. Independent buff
ordering and complete artifact/talent hooks remain outstanding.

Fifteen ordinary thrown weapons use Ballistica, strength requirements, Sharpshooting,
augmentation and hit durability. Stack wear, pinning, recovery and group
identity are stored. Heavy boomerangs return after five local turns, recover
at the throw position or attack an enemy occupying it. Shuriken's free throw
starts a cooldown without resetting it on later throws.

The Cleric has a paged list of 27 spells plus five passive talents. Tier points,
Priest/Paladin and armor-ability branches gate learning. Trinity remembers an
identified type found during the run: imprinting spends tome charge and using
an imprint spends armor charge. Light Reading permits backpack tome use with
reduced recharge; Satiated Spells, Enlightening Meal, Searing Light and Heroic
Energy have runtime hooks. These systems remain partial, especially spells
that depend on more complete combat, terrain or artifact behavior.

The spellbook stores a weighted study order and accepts either of its first
two required scrolls. Learned scrolls offer regular/exotic choices. Scroll
effects share dispatch with physical scroll use and inscription recall.
Recharging is timed; Lullaby applies Drowsy before magical sleep; Retribution
uses the pinned current-HP scaling and applies blindness and weakness to the
reader. Metamorphosis remains unavailable because the other class talent
trees are incomplete; attempting it retains the scroll.

V15 retains the frozen V12–V14 entity prefixes and appends `EXT1`. It stores
plant/spell effects, ghost gear, Cleric progression, Trinity imprints, discovered
types, spellbook order and Foresight awareness. V9–V14 import paths remain; independently generated
historical runtime fixtures currently cover V12/V13. Earlier unpublished V15
snapshots are not a supported interchange format. Native value layouts remain
platform-specific. Invalid extended state is rejected before replacing a save.

Validation on the development tree:

- `bash tools/check-frost.sh`: 10,001 Chill speed cases match pinned Java;
  Frost water durations and detach constants are checked against pinned sources.
  Runtime tests cover independent paralysis, water thaw, zero-damage thaw,
  movement blocking, expiry and saving.

- `bash tools/check-hourglass.sh`: 44,000 charge, turns-to-cost and expiry
  states match pinned Java across 1..10 charges and fractional/oversized actions.
  Azahar scene 123 covers incremental charge, save/load and manual cancellation.
  Stasis adds 4,510 extracted Java cases for charge, duration and hunger limits;
  host tests cover continuing healing/status clocks and protection from damage.

- `bash tools/check-time-bubble.sh`: 93,600 states match extracted pinned Java
  `reset`/`processTime`, including fractional actions and expiry tolerance.
  Host tests cover queued presses, save/load, offensive detachment, Sandals
  activation order, transition disarming and monster-local time consumption.
  Azahar scene 122 passes zero-budget retention, queued trap deduplication,
  save/load and expiry-triggered activation on the rebuilt ARM binary.

- `bash tools/check-plants.sh` compares 26,650 Earthroot absorption cases,
  18,000 Sungrass ticks, nine cure entries and three buff durations against
  extracted pinned Java methods. Host tests cover shared potion cleansing,
  independent anchors, movement-only Cripple, refresh preservation and saving.

- The host ASan/UBSan runtime suite covers projectile wear/recovery/pinning,
  seed feeding and anchors, theft, ghost equipment/XP isolation, tome gates,
  Trinity costs, backpack tome use, scroll effects, cross-floor stasis/link
  ownership, deterministic continuation, truncation and failed-save retention.
  Foresight tests cover mapping, discovery XP, actor/heap sensing, cross-floor
  expiry, repeat scans, Priest illumination, charge debt and its save round-trip.
- `bash tools/check-foresight.sh` compares 12,072 cases against pinned
  `ConeAOE`, `Ballistica`, `PointF` and extracted Talisman methods: exact cone
  cells, target extension/clamping and whole/fractional charge consumption.
- `bash tools/check-projectiles.sh` compares 30,690 cases against extracted
  pinned Java methods: damage ranges, strength and durability for all fifteen
  ordinary missiles, levels +0..30, rings, augmentation and Crystal. This checks
  those formulas, not complete projectile runtime behavior.
- Both 3DSX and CIA build locally. Azahar scenes 117/118/119 pass cross-floor
  ghost/link/save state and Trinity/talent menus. Scenes 115/116/109/106/108/105
  pass the artifact/enchantment menus and prior floor/combat checks. Scene 87
  passes 800 floors with zero generation/rule/room-weight failures, 341 Loop,
  299 FigureEight and zero ordinary-floor fallbacks. The real save hash is
  unchanged and disposable slots are absent.

Outstanding differences include Metamorphosis and other class talent trees,
complete elemental terrain and item destruction, independent buff scheduling,
all tipped darts, full wand behavior, exact loot/value decks, some artifact
active effects, and several high-tier spell interactions. Foresight retains
native heap identity and buff-clock differences; Light Wall uses temporary features, and Trinity
artifact actions reuse partial native effects. Hardware testing is pending.

## 中文

本开发版尚未发布。已补入 13 种神器目录、17 种武器附魔、10 种武器诅咒、
13 种护甲刻印、8 种护甲诅咒、十二种种子和十五种普通投掷武器。
目录齐全不代表所有机制已与固定 v4.0.2 一致。

本轮修正皇室花/治疗药水的九项净化清单、梦夜花 30 回合祝福、致盲草仅减慢
移动的残废、阳光草/地缚根独立锚点及最后一次治疗/吸收边界。独立 Java 对照
通过 26,650 项吸收和 18,000 次治疗。迅捷蓟已使用独立时间气泡，支持零额度
容差、完整动作消耗、延迟机关、进攻解除、离层处理和存档；93,600 项独立
Java 状态对照通过。守望者、完整冰冻、元素物品破坏、原版独立视觉调度和
部分主动效果解除时机仍有差异。净化不会清除已经生效的魔法睡眠。

沙漏冻结改为发动先扣一次、此后每两点行动逐次扣费，提前解除保留剩余充能。
44,000 项 Java 对照验证充能、行动余量和解除边界；支持延迟机关与小数余量存档。
时间静止已补饥饿补偿，并让已有状态、治疗和怪物状态继续计时，英雄免受伤害。
4,510 项 Java 对照验证消耗、持续时间和饥饿边界；独立状态顺序和完整神器/天赋
交互仍待移植。局外系统不在后续对齐目标内。

冰冠花现使用独立冰冻：伤害触发解冻，水中留下 5 回合寒冷，原有麻痹独立保留。
10,001 项寒冷减速公式对照通过。药水破碎、肉类冻结和完整免疫/抗性仍待移植。

自然之履按 3/6/9 种不同种子培养；袖章可对选中商品尝试偷窃；玫瑰支持
装备交付和幽灵指挥。幽灵、光之盟友与附近虹卫可跨层，停滞与生命联结
重新绑定到目标层的召唤物。普通镜像留在原楼层。投掷武器支持整组磨损、
钉入、死亡掉落和同组回收，重型回旋镖五回合返回并可攻击返回位置的敌人。

牧师菜单包含 27 个法术和五个非施法天赋，按层级、转职和护甲能力解锁。
三位一体区分法典灌注与护甲发动，并记住本局发现的物品类型。轻便阅读
可在背包中使用法典。魔典保存学习顺序，并提供普通/异化选择；卷轴与
魔典、铭文召回共用效果入口。蜕变依赖尚未完成的其他职业天赋树，目前
无法使用且不会消耗卷轴。

先见护符改用原版锥形射线，支持近邻延长、距离截断、小数充能欠额、发现经验、
敌人跟踪和按楼层记录的物品堆感知。离层后感知仍会过期，读档保留剩余时间。
陷阱警示要求视野内存在隐藏陷阱，重复停留不会每回合刷提示。物品堆仍按原生
格子模型处理，同格新物品堆身份与独立状态调度尚未完整移植。

存档为 V15，继续导入 V9–V14；真实旧版程序生成的主机迁移样本覆盖 V12/V13。
新增主机回归包括跨层停滞/联结、确定性续跑、三位一体消耗、幽灵击杀经验
与英雄状态隔离、取消选择、回旋镖返回和无效扩展存档保留旧文件。
30,690 项独立 Java 对照验证普通投掷武器的伤害、力量和耐久基础公式。
另有 12,072 项独立 Java 对照验证先见护符的锥形格子、截断和小数充能消耗；
Azahar 场景 120 验证探查、感知、充能、保存/读回及过期。
新一轮 Azahar 场景 117/118/119、115/116、109/106/108/105 均通过；
800 层生成检查零失败。真实存档哈希未变，临时测试槽已清理。

仍未完整对齐的部分包括蜕变、元素地形与物品破坏、独立状态时序、全部
特殊飞镖、法杖原版行为、先见物品堆身份、部分神器主动效果和高阶法术交互。
