新生成的普通层会把钥匙、食物、力量药水和升级卷轴放在玩家从入口经普通门可达的位置。普通房没有合适的格子时，必需补给可放到其他可达位置。如果宝箱会截断通路，战利品会直接放在地上。

Azahar 的 800 层检查全部通过，包括楼梯、各类钥匙和每层计划补给。3DSX 已在 Azahar 运行，CIA 已通过安装验证。现有存档可继续读取，下楼生成新地图时会采用新规则。

## English

New regular floors place keys, food, potions of strength, and scrolls of upgrade where the hero can reach them from the entrance through ordinary doors. Required supplies can use other reachable cells if ordinary rooms have no eligible space. If a chest would cut off a reachable area, its loot appears on the ground.

The 800-floor Azahar audit passed, including stairs, typed keys, and each floor's planned supplies. The 3DSX ran in Azahar and the CIA installed successfully. Existing saves can be loaded, and the placement rules apply when a new floor is generated.
