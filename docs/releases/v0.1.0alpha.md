## 简体中文

首个公开 Alpha 版。游戏逻辑、界面、输入、存档和渲染均通过 libctru / citro2d 原生运行，不依赖 JVM 或 APK 封装。

- 可从第 1 层玩到第 25 层，包含五个区域、五位首领和尤格-泽瓦终战。支持六位英雄、设置、本地排行榜和存档槽。
- 实现回合制战斗、视野、陷阱、暗门和楼梯；收录 90 种物品，包括装备、法杖、药水、卷轴、戒指和任务物品。
- 第 6、11、16 层设有商店，第 20 层的小鬼商店需先完成任务。支持买卖、回购，以及店主警告与离开机制。
- 包含幽魂、制杖师、铁匠和小鬼任务，以及炼金、嬗变卷轴物品选择、安卡复活和简化天赋。
- 存档格式为 V9。物品图标对照原版图集核查，CIA 带自定义横幅和短提示音。

**已知限制：**暂无游戏内音乐和音效；天赋尚非完整职业树；部分墙体前景仍为近似实现；旧矿洞是同层封闭区域，并非独立子地图。升级前建议备份存档。

## English

The first public alpha of the native C++17 port. Game logic, UI, input, saves, and rendering run directly on the 3DS with libctru and citro2d. No JVM or APK wrapper is required.

- A 25-floor run across five regions, five bosses, and the Yog-Dzewa finale. Six heroes, settings, local rankings, and save slots.
- Turn-based combat, field of view, traps, secret doors, and stairs. 90 items, including equipment, wands, potions, scrolls, rings, and quest items.
- Shops on floors 6, 11, and 16, plus the Imp's quest-gated shop on floor 20. Buying, selling, buyback, and shopkeeper warnings and departure.
- Ghost, Wandmaker, Blacksmith, and Imp quests, plus alchemy, a Scroll of Transmutation item picker, Ankh revival, and simplified talents.
- V9 saves. Item sprites checked against the upstream atlas; the CIA has a custom banner and short jingle.

**Known limitations:** no in-game music or sound effects; simplified talent trees; approximate wall foreground layers; Old Mine is a sealed area on the same floor, not a separate sublevel. Back up your saves before upgrading.
