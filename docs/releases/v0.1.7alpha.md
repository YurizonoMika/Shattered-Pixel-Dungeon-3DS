## 简体中文

- 武器攻击按命中与闪避判定。伏击可以绕过普通闪避，护甲随机抵消物理伤害，最低可减到 0 点。精准和闪避指环分别影响命中和闪避。
- 调整部分敌人的基础生命值。怨灵只有 1 点生命，宝箱怪随楼层加深而增强。首领使用当前移植版的阶段和数值。
- 敌人和英雄死亡时使用通用倒下淡出动画。英雄动画播完后才显示结算框。护盾和安卡在致死伤害结算时生效。

旧存档可继续读取，已生成的敌人会保留存档中的生命值。新版数值用于之后生成的敌人。

## English

- Weapon attacks roll accuracy against evasion. Surprise attacks bypass ordinary evasion, and armor can reduce physical damage to zero. Accuracy and evasion rings affect their respective rolls.
- Revised base health for selected enemies. Wraiths have 1 HP and mimic health scales with depth. Bosses use the port's current encounter phases and balance.
- Enemies and the hero fall and fade on death. The hero's game-over dialog appears after the animation. Shields and Ankhs are resolved when lethal damage lands.

Existing saves remain readable. Previously generated enemies retain their saved health. Updated stats apply to newly spawned enemies.
