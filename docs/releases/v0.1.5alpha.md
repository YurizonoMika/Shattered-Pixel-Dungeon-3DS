## 简体中文

修复深渊误用水岸图块而出现的黑色条纹。深渊现在使用原版图块，水流到深渊边缘时也显示正确的衔接。地图生成、通行规则和存档格式未改动，旧存档直接读取即可看到修复。

已在 Azahar 验证监狱第 7 层的深渊走廊，以及独立场景中的水面滚动和水与深渊交界。CIA 安装成功。

## English

Fixed striped chasms caused by drawing water shoreline tiles in place of chasm sprites. Chasms now use the original sprites, including the edge where water meets a drop. Generation, movement rules, and the save format are unchanged. The fix also applies to existing floors on load.

Verified the floor 7 Prison walkway, scrolling water, and the water-to-chasm edge in Azahar. The CIA installed successfully.
